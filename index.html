I’m a QA Lead with 8+ years of experience in high-coverage, CI-driven test automation for SaaS and product companies.

I started as a junior QA engineer at a cryptocurrency exchange, testing trading algorithms for performance. Since then I have worked on healthcare systems, SaaS products serving U.S. clients and large automation suites. Today I lead QA at CREST InfoSystems in Surat.

My work aims to reduce production defects and operational toil by shifting quality left and building it into DevOps pipelines. More and more, that work includes AI. I use Claude Code, GitHub Copilot and LLM prompt libraries to speed up test development, framework modernisation, debugging, code review, test-data generation and documentation, and the guardrails I set keep a human in charge of every result.

I hold a Master of Science in Computer Applications from UKA Tarsadia University and am ISTQB CTFL 4.0, AWS and Azure certified.

§ 01 — How I work

Three habits that show up in every project.

i.

Shift left, wire it in.

Quality checks belong in the pipeline and should run on every commit, so problems are found while they’re still cheap to fix.

ii.

Measure the change.

Coverage, cycle time, defects that escape to production, time-to-first-PR. If a quality effort doesn’t move a number, it gets re-examined.

iii.

AI drafts, humans decide.

AI-generated code is always reviewed by a person. Production data stays out of prompts, licences and PII are scanned, and coverage thresholds stop the suite from bloating.
